    Verify a published check

    Every check Netra publishes is anchored in the ClaimRegistry contract on Polygon Amoy (testnet). Anyone can recompute the report from the open data and confirm it matches the record. Each claim commits to the registry serial ranges behind it; if one range shows up in two claims, anyone can prove it on-chain and the later claim is flagged.

    Method

    How Netra scores a buyer

    1. Retirements. Every Verra and Gold Standard retirement since 2020 from the Berkeley Voluntary Registry Offsets Database (v2026-08, CC BY 4.0), grouped by the named beneficiary.
    2. Project type. Assigned only when three independent sources agree: Berkeley's curated type, CarbonPlan OffsetsDB, and the registry methodology code. Any disagreement gets no research estimate.
    3. Research estimate. Share of credits that achieved real reductions per project type, from Probst et al. 2024 (Nature Communications, 2,346 projects): cookstoves 10.8%, REDD+ 24.7%, HFC-23 68.3%, wind in China and US improved forest management 0%. Types without a published estimate are shown as "no estimate", never filled in.
    4. Grade. Likely-real share of research-covered tonnes: A ≥ 60%, B 40–60%, C 20–40%, D 10–20%, E < 10%. No grade when research covers under a quarter of a buyer's tonnes.
    5. Integrity labels. Each methodology's status from the ICVCM Core Carbon Principles assessment (snapshot 18 Aug 2026).

    Netra shows risk estimates based on average research findings per project type. It is not a verdict on any individual project or company. Registry data are shown as published; a beneficiary name may be a broker or a brand rather than the claimant.
